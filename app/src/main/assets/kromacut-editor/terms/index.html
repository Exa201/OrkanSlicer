Check every model before printing

Kromacut's previews, color predictions, hiding-distance calculations, calibration results, and print instructions are aids to planning. They do not guarantee an exact physical color match, a successful print, or suitability for a particular purpose. Materials, lighting, calibration, printer setup, and slicer settings affect the result.

Inspect each exported STL or 3MF in your slicer. Check dimensions, geometry, orientation, layer heights, first-layer height, nozzle and extrusion settings, filament assignments, and swaps before starting the printer. Recheck after changing settings or updating the app, and use a small test print where appropriate.

Follow your printer and filament manufacturers' safety instructions. An export is not an engineering assessment or safety certification. Any safety-critical, load-bearing, medical, or other regulated use needs independent checks appropriate to that use.

Local data and backups

Kromacut processes artwork on your device and does not provide cloud project storage or a recovery service for your local files. Browser or app storage can be cleared, lost, or become unavailable.

Keep your original images and export backups of important palettes, filament profiles, and calibration history, especially before clearing storage, changing devices, or updating the app. Save exported models somewhere you can find again. The operator cannot remotely recover data held only on your device.
