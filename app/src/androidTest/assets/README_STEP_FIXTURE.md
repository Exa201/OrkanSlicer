# STEP import fixture attribution

`step_cube_ccby4.step` is the 10 mm cube sample from `takuto-NA/step-standards-explained`,
`examples/cube.step`. It is included solely as a controlled native import regression fixture.

Source: https://github.com/takuto-NA/step-standards-explained/blob/main/examples/cube.step

`named_compound_bodies.step` and `named_single_body.step` are the STEP import-name regression
fixtures from OrcaSlicer commit `fa5f7e3db4` (`tests/data/`). They cover named BREP bodies,
including UTF-8 names, split compounds, and a single printable body. OrcaSlicer is licensed
under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0; these small test fixtures are retained with
their upstream source and attribution for regression testing.
